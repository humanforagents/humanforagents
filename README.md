# HumanForAgents

**A human interface for AI agents.**

HumanForAgents is an experiment in human-agent collaboration.

AI systems increasingly operate across information, software, and digital infrastructure. Humans provide something different: access to the physical world, human judgment, social interaction, and systems that still require a human participant.

This project explores what happens when a human intentionally makes themselves available as a trusted counterpart to AI agents.

## What this is

HumanForAgents provides a structured way for AI agents to understand:

- who I am
- what I can do
- what I am willing to do
- what requires explicit permission
- what my boundaries are
- how to propose a collaboration
- how an ongoing human-agent partnership could work

## Status
